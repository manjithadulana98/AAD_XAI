================================================================================
C. CHANNEL IMPORTANCE METHOD
================================================================================

Three complementary methods were used:

  (a) CHANNEL OCCLUSION: Replace each channel's values with zero across
      all time points. Measure change in P(attended). Positive ΔP means
      removing the channel HURTS decoding (facilitatory channel).
      Negative ΔP means removing the channel HELPS (suppressive channel).

  (b) CHANNEL PERMUTATION: Shuffle each channel's values across windows
      (breaking temporal alignment while preserving marginal statistics).
      Same interpretation as occlusion.

  (c) INTEGRATED GRADIENTS (supporting only): Gradient-based attribution
      using a zero baseline. Ranks channels by mean |attribution|.
      Used as corroborating evidence only, not for significance testing.

Occlusion–Permutation correlation: r = 0.947
Channels with CI-based occlusion significance (excludes 0): 44/64
Channels with CI-based permutation significance (excludes 0): 40/64

D. MULTIPLE-COMPARISON CORRECTION
================================================================================

To control for testing 64 channels simultaneously, Benjamini-Hochberg
FDR correction was applied to sign-flip permutation p-values.

Sign-flip permutation p-value estimation: For each channel, per-window ΔP
values were sign-flipped 5000 times under the null of zero mean.
A two-sided p-value was computed from |mean(ΔP)| versus the null distribution.

FDR threshold: α = 0.05

Robust significance criteria (all must hold):
  1. Occlusion and permutation agree in sign (both positive or both negative)
  2. At least one method (occlusion or permutation) is FDR-significant
  3. Subject-wise stability ≥ 50%

================================================================================
I. LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

1. SAMPLE SIZE: Analysis used N=8100 windows. While bootstrap CIs
   and FDR correction provide statistical control, larger N is recommended
   for definitive conclusions (N≥500 for publication-quality results).

2. UNATTENDED ENVELOPE PROXY: The DTU dataset provides only the attended
   envelope. The 'unattended' envelope is a circular time-shift proxy.
   Results may differ with a true competing-speaker paradigm.

3. ELECTRODE MONTAGE: A montage file was provided, but channel-to-electrode
   assignments should be independently verified against the DTU dataset
   documentation before publication.

4. INTEGRATED GRADIENTS: IG uses a zero baseline, which may not be
   neurophysiologically meaningful. IG results are supporting evidence only
   and are not used for significance testing.

5. SUBJECT-WISE STABILITY: Limited by windows per subject. Individual
   channel importance profiles are noisy with small samples.

6. CROSS-SUBJECT CONSISTENCY: Mean pairwise r = 0.037 ± 0.262.
   Low inter-subject agreement indicates subject-specific importance patterns.
   Group-level channel maps may obscure individual differences.

7. FDR CORRECTION: Sign-flip permutation p-values are finite-sample estimates
   with resolution 1/(5000+1). Increasing n_perm improves resolution
   at the cost of computation time.

8. FREQUENCY INTERPRETATION: Frequency-band interpretation, especially
   delta-band effects, should be treated cautiously because the analysis window is short.
